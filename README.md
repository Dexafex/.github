# Dexafex/.github

Organisation-wide files for [github.com/Dexafex](https://github.com/Dexafex).

- `profile/README.md` is the public introduction shown on the organisation page.

The risk warning, company details and restricted jurisdictions in the profile are copied word for word
from the footer of [dexafex.com](https://dexafex.com). When the website's wording changes, change it here too.

The images in `profile/assets/` are rendered by `tools/render.cjs` (light and dark, at twice display size) from the
brand mark and wordmark in `tools/brand/`. Every sentence on them is the wording of dexafex.com. To re-render:
`NODE_PATH=<a node_modules that holds playwright> node tools/render.cjs`.
